The Dewey Decimal System

How the shelves are arranged

Every book gets a number for its subject. The first digit is the main class, one shelf each; the next two narrow it down, and anything after the decimal point is more specific still. Within a shelf, books go in number order, then by the author’s last name. The numbers on the right are how many of my books are there.
